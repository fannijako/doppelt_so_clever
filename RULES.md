# Doppelt So Clever (Twice as Clever)

The clever dice game by Wolfgang Warsch for 1-4 players, ages 8+.

## Game Overview

Choose your dice twice as cleverly in **Doppelt So Clever**, marking them in the matching colored areas on your Scoring sheet, outting together chain scoring opportunities, and racking up points! The dice you don't use are as important as those you do, because every die that has a lower value than your chosen die can be used by the other players! The player with the most points at the end wins!

## Setup

Each player receives a Felt-tip pen and a Score sheet. Randomly choose a starting player, who picks up the 6 dice.

## Gameplan

The game is played over four (4 players), five (3 players) or six (2 players) rounds. At the start of the first four rounds, each player gets one bonus, as shown on the Round trace on the Score sheet.

## Active player

The **active player** reveives all the dice. They roll all 6 at once, choose a **die**, placing it onto one of their 3 Dice fields (top left of **Scoring sheet**), without changing the number of the die.

Using the chosen die, the player must now use the value and the color to mark on their Score sheet a corresponding number in the same color area (see Score sheet).

The **white die** is a **wild color** and can be used in place of any other color.

Then the active player must place **all dice that show a lower value** than the currently selected die onto the **Silver Platter** printed in the box. If the active player selects the lowest value die, no dice are placed onto the Silver Platter. Dice on the Silver Platter can no longer be used by the active player (exception: **Additional Dice action**).
